# Surface: Loja virtual (home, catálogo, produto, carrinho, checkout)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: women shopping on mobile first. Action: choose a variant and buy.
Constraints: owner-pinned palette, serif+sans pairing, script only on "brilhar", gold as detail only. No product photos yet (owner decision): designed placeholders carry the grid until uploads arrive. No invented ratings, review counts or customer numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A boutique window, not a marketplace grid. Refuses the stock e-commerce hero-banner-plus-carousel; the page reads like the shop's own lookbook where every piece shows its real availability by variant.
OWN-WORLD: Sálvia header band, off-white and creme grounds, rosé and bege category fields, olive primary actions, gold hairlines/script/stars-free details; Cormorant Garamond display, Manrope interface; 16–24px radii, whisper shadows, beige hairline borders; category-tinted product frames with fine-line drawings when no photo exists.
STORY: Visitor understands this is Use Gazzeta (moda, make, perfume), believes pieces are curated and in stock, picks a color and size and adds to cart in two taps.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sálvia header (wordmark, search "O que você procura hoje?", conta/favoritos/sacola), menu row; hero split: left editorial headline at display scale with "brilhar" in gold script, subtext, olive COMPRAR AGORA pill, three benefit lines; right a staggered trio of featured product frames (dress, perfume, make) standing like a shop window, becoming photos when uploaded.
FORM: Owner-pinned direction from five mockups (brief pin beats roll). Seed key f49571fe; challengers fight-poster, sukeban dress code, drum-machine step row, cracktro queue, provenance ribbon declined on audience identification and product clarity. Raise kept from provenance ribbon: every variant's stock carries a readable custody line of movements in the panel.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
